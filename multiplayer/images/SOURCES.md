# Choice images

The image shown for each choice of the NelfePlay MULTIPLAYER panel (character, vehicle, team), referenced by the `image`
field of a choice value in `catalogue.json`. Cabinets install them with the rest of `multiplayer/` and draw them from disk.
Each image is a square PNG at the game's own pixel size; the panel scales it up with nearest-neighbour filtering.

These images reproduce artwork of the games themselves and remain the property of their respective rights holders. They
are not APIExpose data and are not covered by `DATA-LICENSE.md`.

Two sources:

- the public badge of a RetroAchievements achievement (`https://media.retroachievements.org/Badge/<badge>.png`);
- a square cut from the game's own select screen, captured under MAME.
